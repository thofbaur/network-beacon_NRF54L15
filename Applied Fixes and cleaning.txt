- Initial postprocessing filtered out beacon IDs with 0x48 to 0x57 as first id, and treated them as literal numbers. All logs until 15.8.2026 21:00 o'clock map ids 48 to 57 onto 0 to 9. After that ids were treated normally. In early logs, the ids 0 to 9 were seaparated comparing the reported and calculated 0 time of the report. Reports after 16.8.2026 0:00 have correct ids and can be used as indicators. Reports with the ids in second place (from other beacons) were used to check if an Id was already present at a time.
